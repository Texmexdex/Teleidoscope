Privacy Policy

Tautological Teleidescope · Last updated: August 31, 2026

Tautological Teleidescope ("the app") is developed by TeXmExDeX Type Tools. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: the app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

The app collects no personal information. We do not gather names, email addresses, location, device identifiers, contacts, or any other personal data.

How your images are handled

Any photo or image you open in the app is processed entirely on your own device. Your images are never uploaded to any server and are never transmitted off your device. When you close the app, they are not retained by us.

Data storage

The app saves your control settings (such as grid size and color options) locally on your device so it can remember your preferences. This information stays on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Network access

The app functions fully offline. It does not require an account and does not transmit your content or usage data. It may load a display font from a public font service for styling; no personal or image data is involved in that request.

Third-party services

The app does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking of any kind.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to TeXmExDeX Type Tools at texmexdex11@gmail.com.
